<p>Earning a computer science or information technology degree shouldn’t mean sitting through classes to relearn skills you have already mastered in the workforce. If you hold active, industry-standard credentials, securing <strong>IT certification college credit</strong> is one of the smartest ways to skip redundant coursework, save thousands in tuition, and accelerate your graduation timeline.</p>

<p><em>(Before you apply, ensure your technical foundation is solid and your certifications are up to date by exploring the expert-led programs on our <a href="/courses/">AIIT Network courses page</a>.)</em></p>

<p>By leveraging Prior Learning Assessments (PLA) or Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL), many globally accessible universities will recognize your expertise and convert it directly into academic credit.</p>

<p>Here are 10 international universities where you can successfully claim IT certification college credit for your professional tech credentials.</p>

<h3>1. Western Governors University (WGU) – USA (Global Online)</h3>

<p>WGU is famous for its competency-based education model. Instead of logging hours in a classroom, you prove what you know. WGU has direct mapping for tech credentials, meaning the CompTIA A+, Cisco CCNA, or AWS Certified Solutions Architect can automatically clear entire courses, making it a top destination for IT certification college credit.</p>

<ul>
<li>
<strong>Helpful Link:</strong> <a href="https://www.wgu.edu/admissions/transfers/wgu-transcript-request/transferable-certifications.html">WGU Transferable IT Certifications</a>
</li>
</ul>

<h3>2. The Open University – United Kingdom</h3>

<p>As one of Europe’s largest distance-learning institutions, The Open University is highly accessible to international students. Through their Credit Transfer system, they frequently map professional IT training (including specific Cisco networking and AWS cloud credentials) into their computing and IT bachelor’s degrees.</p>

<ul>
<li>
<strong>Helpful Link:</strong> <a href="https://www.open.ac.uk/study/credit-transfer/">The Open University Credit Transfer</a>
</li>
</ul>

<h3>3. Deakin University – Australia</h3>

<p>Deakin University is a strong option for students aiming to study in Australia or online. Through their Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL) framework, professionals can submit portfolios, work experience, and vendor certifications (like Microsoft, IBM, or AWS) to shave time off both undergraduate and postgraduate tech degrees.</p>

<ul>
<li>
<strong>Helpful Link:</strong> <a href="https://www.deakin.edu.au/study/how-to-apply/recognition-of-prior-learning">Deakin Recognition of Prior Learning</a>
</li>
</ul>

<h3>4. University of Maryland Global Campus (UMGC) – USA / Global</h3>

<p>UMGC was built specifically for working adults and military personnel across the globe. They have a streamlined “Fast Paths to Credit” program that awards direct college credit for active industry certifications from Google, CompTIA, Microsoft, and Amazon.</p>

<ul>
<li>
<strong>Helpful Link:</strong> <a href="https://www.umgc.edu/transfers-and-credits/fast-paths-to-credit/industry-certification-professional-courses">UMGC Industry Certification Credit</a>
</li>
</ul>

<h3>5. Athabasca University – Canada</h3>

<p>Known as Canada’s Open University, Athabasca is a leader in online global education. Their Prior Learning Assessment and Recognition (PLAR) process allows tech professionals to submit a portfolio of industry credentials, bootcamps, and workplace training to be evaluated for university credit.</p>

<ul>
<li>
<strong>Helpful Link:</strong> <a href="https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.athabascau.ca/admissions/transfer-credit/prior-learning.html">Athabasca University PLAR</a>
</li>
</ul>

<h3>6. Purdue Global – USA</h3>

<p>Backed by the prestigious Purdue University system, Purdue Global allows students to fast-track their degrees by submitting professional training records. Their IT programs specifically award credit for a wide array of technical certifications and exams that have been evaluated by their faculty or the American Council on Education (ACE).</p>

<ul>
<li>
<strong>Helpful Link:</strong> <a href="https://www.purdueglobal.edu/transfer-students/professional-training/">Purdue Global Professional Training Credit</a>
</li>
</ul>

<h3>7. Charles Sturt University (CSU) – Australia</h3>

<p>CSU is highly regarded for its IT and cybersecurity programs. They offer unique “Industry Certification credit arrangements,” particularly for their Master of Information Technology students. If you hold major tech vendor certifications, you can significantly reduce the number of subjects you need to complete.</p>

<ul>
<li>
<strong>Helpful Link:</strong> <a href="https://www.google.com/search?q=https://study.csu.edu.au/apply/credit-prior-learning">CSU Credit for Prior Learning</a>
</li>
</ul>

<h3>8. Southern New Hampshire University (SNHU) – USA (Global Online)</h3>

<p>SNHU has one of the most generous transfer policies in the world, allowing students to transfer up to 90 credits toward a 120-credit bachelor’s degree. They routinely accept ACE-evaluated IT certifications, meaning your CompTIA Security+ or Google IT Support Certificate can immediately knock out foundational classes.</p>

<ul>
<li>
<strong>Helpful Link:</strong> <a href="https://www.snhu.edu/admission/transferring-credits">SNHU Transfer Credits</a>
</li>
</ul>

<h3>9. Excelsior University – USA</h3>

<p>Excelsior is heavily tailored toward adult learners who want to finish their degrees quickly. They accept up to 113 transfer credits and have deep integrations with the ACE credit recommendation system, making it incredibly easy to turn your cybersecurity and networking certs into a finished degree.</p>

<ul>
<li>
<strong>Helpful Link:</strong> <a href="https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.excelsior.edu/admissions/transfer-more-credits/">Excelsior Transfer Credit</a>
</li>
</ul>

<h3>10. Thomas Edison State University (TESU) – USA</h3>

<p>TESU is a powerhouse for degree completion. They specialize in assessing knowledge acquired outside of a traditional classroom. Through their Prior Learning Assessment (PLA) program, they evaluate corporate training, military experience, and standard IT certifications to award up to 90 credits toward a bachelor’s degree.</p>

<ul>
<li>
<strong>Helpful Link:</strong> <a href="https://www.google.com/search?q=https://www.tesu.edu/degree-completion/pla">TESU Prior Learning Assessment</a>
</li>
</ul>
